How to Choose the Right Packing Tape for Your Business

Packing Tape Guide — First4Tapes

Choosing the wrong packing tape can lead to damaged shipments, wasted money and frustrated customers. Here's everything you need to know to make the right choice.

Whether you're running a small e-commerce store or managing a large warehouse operation, the type of tape you use plays a critical role in keeping your packages secure during transit.

1. Understanding the Types of Packing Tape

There are three main types of packing tape used in commercial packaging — each with distinct advantages depending on your use case.

BOPP (Biaxially Oriented Polypropylene) Tape

2. Key Factors to Consider

  • Package weight — heavier boxes need thicker, stronger tape
  • Environment — humidity and temperature affect adhesion
  • Surface type — corrugated cardboard vs. recycled boxes
  • Budget — bulk orders significantly reduce cost per roll
  • Speed — machine-grade tape for automated packaging lines

3. Our Recommendation

For most businesses, we recommend starting with BOPP transparent tape (48mm x 100m) for general use. If you're shipping heavier items, upgrade to our hot melt range for superior holding strength.
